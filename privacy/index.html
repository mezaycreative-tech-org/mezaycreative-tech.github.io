Privacy

Happy Wife, Happy Husband and Happy Children · last updated 26 October 2026

Almost everything you put into these apps stays on your own phone. There are no ads, nothing is sold, and there is no tracking of any kind.

Who this covers

The three apps at happyrelationships.app — Happy Wife, Happy Husband and Happy Children. They are run by Moshe Mezei (Mezay Creative). You can reach us at momezei@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

By default, everything you do in the apps is saved in your browser's own storage on your phone, and nowhere else:

  • The habits and suggestions you tick off
  • Notes, lists and anything you type
  • Dates, reminders and your streak
  • Your answers to the short setup questions, and your settings

We cannot see any of it. If you clear your browser's site data or delete the app from your home screen, it is gone, and we have no copy to give you back.

What leaves your device

A count when the app is opened

The first time you open an app on a given day, it records one line: which app, the date, and the hour. Nothing else — no name, no account, no device identifier, nothing you typed. It exists so we can tell whether anyone is using these apps at all.

Reminders, if you turn them on

Happy Wife and Happy Children can send reminder notifications. If you switch those on, your browser hands us an anonymous delivery address so the reminder can reach your phone. It is not tied to your name or email. Switching reminders off removes it.

An account, if you choose to make one

Happy Wife and Happy Husband let you create an account so your things carry over to another phone. This is optional — the apps work fully without one. If you do create one, we store your email address, a securely hashed password, and the app content you chose to sync. Happy Children has no accounts at all.

Anything you email us

The feedback button opens your own mail app. If you write to us, we see what you wrote and the address you wrote from, the same as any email.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database, the accounts and the reminder delivery. Their privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages serves the apps themselves. Their privacy statement.

As with any website, their servers see ordinary connection information such as your IP address when your phone loads a page. We do not store it or use it for anything.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone
  • No analytics or tracking tools — no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no third-party trackers of any kind
  • No profiles built about you, and nothing followed across other sites

Children

These apps are written for adults — husbands, wives and parents. Happy Children is a tool for a parent, not something for a child to use. The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Getting your data out, or deleted

  • On your phone: clearing the site's data in your browser settings deletes everything the app has stored locally.
  • Reminders: turn them off in the app's settings and the delivery address is removed.
  • An account: email momezei@gmail.com and we will delete the account and everything stored with it.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.
